# Dragon Island: Risks You Should Understand (draft)

Last updated: `[DATE]` · Version `[VERSION]`

Dragon Island is a game. Some features let you receive and spend $DRIS, a token on Robinhood Chain. Please read
this before you deposit, spend or withdraw tokens. If you do not understand these risks, do not use those features.

## Testnet beta

- The beta runs on Robinhood Chain **testnet**. Testnet DRIS and testnet ETH have **no value**, cannot be sold, and
  will **not** be converted into any future mainnet token. Progress, balances and dragons may be reset at any time.

## Rewards are not guaranteed

- Gold sell-back, season prizes and referral credits are game features, not income, and are **not guaranteed**.
  You may receive nothing.
- Rewards are paid only from tokens the game's vault holds. The vault can run low or empty. At most 2% of its
  balance can be paid out per UTC day across all players; when that is used up, withdrawals wait for the next day.
- The size of a season's prize pool depends on what other players spend during that season. It is not known in
  advance and may be zero.
- We can change rates, limits, fees, prices and rules, and can pause or end any feature, including withdrawals.
  `[COUNSEL: notice obligations.]`
- Accounts that break the Terms can be banned and lose tokens earned in the game.

## $DRIS is not an investment

- Do not buy or hold DRIS expecting a profit. We make no promise about its price, liquidity or future use.
- DRIS may have no market at all. Where a market exists, the price can move sharply and **can fall to zero**.
- Rewards are set in US dollars but paid in DRIS at the market price, so a falling price means the vault pays out
  more tokens and empties faster.
- Burns reduce the number of tokens; that does not mean the price will rise.
- 10% of the supply belongs to the project team and becomes available over 12 months; 50% is intended for a public
  market at mainnet launch. Sales by any holder can move the price.

## Spending is final

- Tokens spent in the store or on buildings are used up. Half of every spend is burned. `[COUNSEL: refund rules.]`
- Deposits cost a 2% fee and withdrawals a 5% fee, both taken in DRIS. Blockchain transactions also cost network fees
  in ETH. The one-time entry payment is not refundable. `[COUNSEL: confirm.]`

## Chance and paid advantages

- Breeding results are random. The game shows the exact odds before you breed; you can still get a common result
  many times in a row (after 10 common results in a row for the same element pair, the next breed skips the common tier if a rarer one is possible).
- A breeding odds boost improves the odds of one breed. It does not guarantee a rarer dragon.
- Players who spend can level up and breed faster and may win more battles. Spending does not guarantee prizes.

## Technology risks

- Smart contracts can contain bugs. `[An independent audit has not yet been completed / was completed by ...]`
  An exploit could lose vault funds.
- The vault has no emergency withdrawal function. Tokens leave it only through signed withdrawal vouchers or
  burns, and both are controlled by keys we hold. A compromised or misused project key could pay out up to 2% of
  the vault per day to any address, or burn vault tokens; our main safeguard is a multi-signature wallet for the
  owner role. If the game shuts down, there is no promise of a final payout.
- Your in-game balance is a record on our server, not tokens in your wallet, until you withdraw. Server outages,
  bugs or attacks can delay or prevent withdrawals. A withdrawal voucher expires after one hour if unused.
- Blockchain transactions are irreversible. Sending tokens to the wrong address, or to a fake copy of the DRIS token
  or vault, cannot be undone. Use only the addresses on our official page.
- You alone control your wallet. If you lose its keys or recovery phrase, nobody can recover your account or
  tokens. We will never ask for your recovery phrase.
- Robinhood Chain is operated by third parties. The project is not affiliated with Robinhood `[COUNSEL: wording]`.
  Outages, bridge failures or changes to the chain are outside our control.
