## 2. What we collect and why

### 2.1 Waitlist site

| Data | Where it comes from | Why | `[COUNSEL: lawful basis]` |
|---|---|---|---|
| X (Twitter) user id, handle, display name, profile image URL, account creation date | X sign-in (read-only permissions; we never post for you and do not keep your X access token) | Identify you on the waitlist; reject very new accounts to limit bots | |
| Wallet address and the time you signed to prove it | A message you sign in your wallet (no transaction, no fee) | Link your wallet to your waitlist place so we can invite you | |
| Times you opened and confirmed each X task (follow, like, repost, comment) | Your clicks on the site; we do not check X to verify them | Show your progress; check the steps needed to join | |
| Your invite code, who invited you, your position and chosen element | Generated by the site | Run the waitlist and invite counts | |
| IP address | Your connection | Short-term rate limiting against abuse; held in memory only, not stored in the database | |
| Session cookies | Set by the site | Keep you signed in | |

We may export the list (handle, wallet, element, invite count, task times, join time) to choose and contact beta
players. We do not sell it.

### 2.2 The game

| Data | Why | `[COUNSEL: lawful basis]` |

| Season Points, trophies, ranking | Seasons and prizes; public leaderboards show your shortened wallet address | |
| Ban status and reason | Enforce the Terms | |
| Your country, derived from your IP by our CDN, checked at request time and not stored | Comply with country restrictions | |
| Which version of the Terms you accepted (once Terms acceptance is switched on) | Record your agreement | |

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or identity documents. `[COUNSEL: update if KYC is introduced.]`

### 2.3 Browser storage

The game stores in your browser's local storage a session token (to keep you signed in) and a random device id
(see the referral check above). The waitlist uses cookies for sign-in only. We use no advertising or analytics
cookies. `[COUNSEL: whether a consent banner is needed.]`

## 3. Public blockchain data

Deposits, entry payments and withdrawals are transactions on Robinhood Chain, a public blockchain. Anyone can see
them, and they cannot be changed or deleted by us or anyone else. Erasure requests cannot remove them.
