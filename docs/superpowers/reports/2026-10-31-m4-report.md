## What works (played through the real UI, with the delivered art in the final pass)

- A fresh account opens on Home's centre 4 plots: 21 obstacles on 77 of 256 tiles (30.1 %), both starter dragons in view.
- A sign opens `Buy plot · 100 gold`: Gold falls by 100, +5 XP, 2–4 obstacles spawn, signs move on.
- Clears: Bush 100 Gold, 5 s, +60 XP. Small Rock 500, 50 s, +300. Ancient Tree 25,000, 40 min, +15,000. Two run at once; a third is refused with `Two clears are running`; a build is accepted during two clears.
- A token on a running clear or breed takes its time off, the surplus is lost, and the clear pays once when it finishes. `Speed-ups 15 min × 2 · 1 h × 1 · 3 h × 3` in Settings. A drop toast fired in play (`Found 13 Food`).
- Bridge to Ember Reach at PL 12 for 150,000: Map, panel, buy, Go. A Tree there costs 3,000 (×3). A habitat placed there, and one moved from Home, both agree after a reload.
- Moves: a real drag across a plot border. `Move to Ember Reach` and the other Island buttons fit at 390 px with six islands, all 44 px tall. The current island shows selected. Cancel returns the view and leaves the building unmoved.
- Map: drag, wheel and CDP pinch. Locks, levels and prices read right. Battle from the Map returns to the island, and its level-up effect plays. Ten open-and-close cycles show no frame over 50 ms and a flat heap (259–287 MB).
- Real art: all 10 habitats and the Golden Aerie stand on their diamonds with feet on the face. Crops sit on the soil and don't shrink on ripening. Ground on all six islands has no stripes, seams or cliff breaks, and fog reads as unowned land.
- Taps: the starting Farm, all 12 Bushes at zoom 1, plot signs at both zooms, and a dragon against its platform each open what's under the finger.
- Shop: `No room: clear or buy land` where true, on habitats, Roost, Grove, Farm and decorations. Two paid skips read `Skip Clearing Ancient Tree` and `Skip Clearing Boulder`.
- No page errors, failed requests or unexpected 4xx in any run. `/me` agreed with the next action on every reload.

## Frame time

390×844, a full 16-plot Home with 12 habitats, 6 farms, 41 obstacles, dev build, 8 s runs.

| Scene | CPU | Before: max ms, frames over 50 ms | After: max ms, frames over 50 ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | 4× | 146, 8; 150, 9 | 42, 0; 38, 0; 62, 1; 29, 0 |
| Map | 4× | 146, 8; 183, 8 | 29, 0; 37, 0; 38, 0; 42, 0 |
| Home, Shop open | 4× | 183, 9 | 42, 0; 50, 1 |

Mean about 6 ms throughout. The final playtest found the spikes. A profile found the cause: React re-rendered the whole HUD on the 1 s clock tick, with the `Icon` placeholder glyphs and StrictMode's second render taking most of it. The scene's 1 s `sync` was a smaller, separate task, and now reuses sprites by id. The production build never spiked, before or after (13–33 ms max), so players mostly gain headroom. The ground is one baked texture per island and holding, and survives a lost WebGL context.

- **Art notes:** Home's grass is much more saturated than the new island grounds and fog (for a later art pass). The Ancient Tree's sheet ends in a flat, hard-cut bottom. At capacity 5–6, dragons pile up and hide most of their platform. Map icons look good, but the plot diamond beside each is plain. `ground.plotEdge` was skipped, so plot borders show only as fog edges.
- **Toasts** now sit above the bottom menu; over an open Shop sheet they cover part of a row but take no taps. Same for the battle screen.
- **A request that never returns** locks Confirm, Cancel and the Island buttons in placement until a reload, since the API client has no timeout.
- **Minor, read from code and not reproduced:** a kept badge drawn before the pixel font arrives stays in the fallback font until its label changes, because `sync` no longer redraws it each second. While placing, the desktop cursor can still become a hand.

## Open questions for shiko

Both stay on the GDD's values, per your coordinator's note, until you say otherwise. Numbers are from bot simulations on the real rules, one seed per run.

1. **Gold income runs far above §7.2.** §7.2 says Day 1 Gold is about 750k. Bots on Flash-14 earn 5.0–5.4M on Day 1 at 6 × 30 min, and 15.5–16.7M non-stop; by Day 4, 13.4M a day casual and 29.4M non-stop. M4 didn't change the income code. §5.2's own numbers point the same way: about 136k Gold a day per habitat, ×10 on Flash-14. Island 2 plots cost 2–8 % of Day 1 income, against §5.5's goal of 25–50 %, and a casual bot that buys land gets all of Home, the Bridge and Island 2 on Day 1. The full archipelago stays out of reach. Options: (a) keep them; (b) lower Flash-14's Gold multiplier toward 750k; (c) raise the plot prices for Islands 2–3.
2. **Clearing obstacles pays a lot of XP.** §5.6's XP × 12 comes to 0.6 XP per Gold, against 0.05 for build XP with its one-level cap. One Ancient Tree takes a new player from PL 1 to PL 11, and the starting layout pays 36,420 XP for 60,700 Gold. Day limits hold the pacing, and end-of-day levels didn't change, but the XP bank at Finals grows from 534k to 1.39M casual and 3.77M non-stop for a bot that buys all land and clears. Options: (a) keep §5.6 with R56; (b) add the one-level cap to clears; (c) pay clear XP without the ×12.
3. **Obstacle share of spend (§5.7 asks for 6, 4, 3 %)** depends on the player: 0 % for bots clearing only when blocked, 0.5 % to about 11 % for those clearing everything. Clearing is optional, so no number here tells you what real players will do. The beta will.
4. **Speed-up tokens and burn (Q2 (a)).** Real-hour tokens from drops replace some `$DRIS` skip spending that §7.7 counts on. Watch it in M7's tokenomics.
5. **Phone opening zoom:** keep zoom 2, or zoom 1 with everything visible and smaller?
6. **Still open from M3:** the level-up modal on every rise, and the Day 13 jump, both on defaults. Species names and tri-hybrids remain yours.
