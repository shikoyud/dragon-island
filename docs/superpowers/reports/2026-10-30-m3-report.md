## What works (played through the real UI)

**First pass, before the fixes and art.** Each of these used grants only where noted.

- A fresh player sees Goal 1. Claiming pays +300 XP and +200 Gold, taking PL 1 to 3; the screen reads `Level 1 → 3` and lists what opened; the bar reads `75 / 362 XP`.
- Goals 2–6 took 38 actions and 3.57 minutes of game time with no grants, ending at PL 8, with 6 level-up screens.
- First hybrid start pays +60 XP, first-time Rare hatch 1,920. Terra × Terra pays 0 on start and hatch after the first hybrid. Terra × Flame rolling a hybrid pays 60 and its hatch.
- Dragon at Lv 7: `Upgrade Roost` goes to the Shop. Roost 5,000 Gold, 5-minute build; feed refused during it, then up to Lv 10; `Upgrade Roost` then opens the Roost panel.
- Goals 7–14 pay exactly (grants for the PL 12 start, Food and the 10-species Book); Quests ends on `All goals done`.
- Ascension at PL 30, Tier 4: 10M Gold plus $2.00 (100 → 60 $DRIS in the test) starts an 8 h timer. With too little $DRIS it is refused and nothing changes.
- Day 1 holds at PL 12 with XP kept. Day 2 gives PL 13. Flash-14 Day 12 holds PL 40; Day 13 pays the bank (PL 44 in that run).
- One gate of each of nine kinds is off one level below and on at its PL; raw actions give `LEVEL_TOO_LOW`.
- Sea habitat pays 250 build XP; a War habitat at PL 18 is capped at one level (+9,088).
- 10 reloads: `/me` agrees with the next action, no second level-up screen.

**Final pass, after the fixes, with real art.**

- `ui.goal` shows beside a claimable Goal. `fx.levelUp` rings play above the card and cover no text.
- Roost Tiers 1–5 each draw their own art and open the panel when tapped. A new Roost, Grove or farm on a fresh island leaves room for a habitat.
- The bar at the limit reads `Level limit` at 390 px, with the whole sentence on tap, and the whole sentence at 1440 px.
- A returning player on the next day gets one screen, `Level 12 → 18`, no raw keys; none after closing it and reloading; none in a fresh browser profile.
- An open tab turns with the day with no action: one `/me`, one screen, the bar and the reasons update.
- A stale claim from a second tab shows copy rather than a code. Goals 11 and 14's `Go to` reach the Shop or the Roost.
- No page errors, no failed requests, no unexpected 4xx. The favicon 404 from M1 is gone.

A throwaway walk on the real rules, with no grants, finished all 14 Goals: Flash-14 non-stop in 2.82 h, Flash-14 at 6 × 30 min a day in 12 h, Flash-28 at 6 × 30 min in 24 h. It found no stuck state. The highest PL a Goal needs is 10, below the lowest limit of 12.

## Simulation numbers

Flash-14, end-of-day Player Level, Days 1–12, for casual and non-stop bots alike: **12, 18, 25, 30, 32, 33, 34, 36, 37, 38, 39, 40**. That is §7.2 exactly, because the limit paces everyone. Without the limit, a casual player (6 × 30 min a day) reaches PL 30 at 20 h and PL 40 at 48 h. This was my error in the plan: I wrote that normal players should rarely see the limit, and the simulation showed the opposite. Shiko has decided: keep the values and the limit.

Terra × Terra-Sea Common breeding pays about 9,000 XP/h, because one parent isn't Common. The limit holds it.

## What does not work yet, or is rough

- **Level limit is visible most of the game.** Since it paces normal play, most players sit at a full bar for much of each day. This follows from the decision above.
- **The level-up screen interrupts often early on:** 6 in the first 3.57 minutes of game time. It's a modal. I kept it per the plan; a non-blocking banner for one-level rises would be small to change.
- **PL 40 to 50 in one jump:** a large bank on Day 13 pays at once, since R41 has no limit from Finals on. §7.2 says "40+".
- **Long level-up lists:** a 1 → 30 list now scrolls between the title and Continue. At 390 px the only cue that it scrolls is a line cut at the bottom.
- **At 390 px the limit toast** covers the bottom menu labels for its 5 s.
- **The Roost sprite is small** at 0.5 scale on its 2 cells, until M4 (R33). Tiers are readable, though a closer look helps.
- **Below 1100 px** the bar shows `Level limit`, with the whole sentence on tap.
- A second device re-lists levels already seen, since the last-shown level is per browser. It's harmless.
- No focus trap on the level-up screen, like the other dialogs. To fix together.
