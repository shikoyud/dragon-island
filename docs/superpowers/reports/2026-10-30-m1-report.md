# M0 + M1 report

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-30-gdd-m1-core-loop.md`. Branch `gdd-m1`, base `99693b2`.

## Result

M0 and M1 are built. The core loop runs on GDD data in the real UI: collect Gold until FULL, plant and harvest, feed four times per level, build, buy premium items with DRIS. Seasons, SP, Hoard, gold sell-back and referrals are gone.

## What works (played through the real UI with the delivered art)

- Sign-in: 1,000 Gold, 50 Food, Terra and Flame habitats (R1). The Terminal clock is visible.
- A capped Terra L1 shows FULL and `80,000 / 80,000`; Collect pays exactly 80,000 and no XP.
- A real pointer tap on a gold badge sends exactly one `collectGold` for the right habitat. The coin burst plays once and goes away.
- Dragon Bush: `15s · 50 gold → 60 food`; not ready early; harvest gives +60 Food.
- Feeding: 5 Food per feed at Lv 1, four pips, level up, and a disabled `Upgrade Roost` at Lv 7.
- Sea habitat: locked at PL 1, 5,000 Gold at PL 3.
- Golden Aerie: $5.00 (100 DRIS at the test price), placed, holds any element, real art.
- Clock: with the server shifted +10 d 1 h it read `Finals lock in 3d 22:59:21`.
- Bottom bar: Shop, Breed, Dragon Book, Battle, Wings, Quests, Inbox, with the last three disabled.
- War and Juvenile sprites are real art, not fallbacks.

## What does not work yet

- **GOLD badge is effectively always on.** Gold now accrues by the millisecond, so a badge returns about 1.4 s after a collect. A display threshold would fix it; see Questions.
- **Desktop framing:** at 1440×900 the island sits high and the top habitat runs under the pills and clock.
- **Phone panels** cover 45–80 % of the screen. The Shop shows about six rows and hides the island.
- **Small things:** Shop tab labels are readable but small; a slow connection can make a countdown hop a second or two; the first `GET /api/me` before sign-in logs a 401 in the console (expected).
- **Stale waitlist copy:** it still promises 14-day seasons and Season Points (`Faq.tsx:4`, `Pillars.tsx:27-28`, `layout.tsx:13`, `Hero.tsx:36`, `CardView.tsx:29`, `Joined.tsx:8`, `wallet/config.ts:38`). Left alone because no plan covers marketing copy.
- **Waitlist War art:** the game has it, the waitlist does not, so about 1 in 10 new entrants get a colour block.
