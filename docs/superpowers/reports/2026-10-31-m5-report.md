## What works (played through the real UI, with delivered art in the final pass)

- A fresh account reaches PL 4 in 5 actions, about 24 s. Trials opens. With 2 dragons the node says `Need 3 dragons · buy a Basic in the Shop`, with a link that works.
- Moves: Strike at 1×, 2×, 0.5×, `IMMUNE`, crits, Swap, KO and forced replacement, Tier 3's `Rests this turn`. In every battle compared, HP after each turn on screen matched the server's replayed log.
- Fatigue: HP carries over and rises 6 % a minute by clock; a KO'd dragon returns at 1 HP and is refused until 25 % (`Fights in 4m`). Heal all costs $0.05 in $DRIS, and without $DRIS gets 402, with nothing changed.
- Stars: 1, 2 and 3 on one node. Replays pay 50 % with no XP; the 6th replay of a node in a game day is refused, and a new day restores 5.
- Boss drops, once: Chapter 1 `2 × 15 min` tokens and a saved chest pull, and a token finished a running crop. Chapter 5's `Uncommon Electric egg` entered a full Hatchery, shown `2/1`. Chapter 10: `Trial Champion joined you`.
- Abandon has a confirm with its cost, and leaves the team on 1 HP. Reload resumes the same seed, including at a forced replacement. `Trial in progress` shows on the island after a reload.
- A stale second tab gets one message, `There is no Trial in progress.`, no dead `Try again`, and `Leave` works. A lost final send offers `Try again`, which reuses its idempotency key.
- PvP: a real battle by clicks at 390×844 and 1440×900; trophies matched the server.
- Art: all 10 backdrops, dragons on their platforms, plates, tier pips, stars, Boss crown, chapter cards, real element icons (which M4's `Icon` had never read, now fixed). No fx covers a plate or move; `fx.move.war` at Tier 3 is a red ring about 190 px across for about 0.4 s, hiding its target, and I kept its scale.
- Fits at 390×844, 360×640 and 1440×900 with no scroll; buttons are at least 44 px.
- No page errors. The only 4xx were expected.

Screenshots: `docs/art/m5-battle.png`, `m5-swap.png`, `m5-result.png`, `m5-desktop.png`, and `m5-trials.png`, which predates the last layout commit. That commit didn't touch the Trials screen.

## What does not work yet, or is rough

- **First Trial is tough:** three low Commons with no counter open, against a Terra, Terra, Sea team. The route is clear, but nothing tells a player to feed first. The Terra immunity costs most.
- **`IMMUNE` text on an adult** rests near the horns, since the title row leaves about 16 px. A clean fix needs a taller stage.
- **Spare room above the stage** on a tall phone and on desktop: a flat field. At 1440×900 the battle is a 360 px column, per R80.
- **PvP has no arena backdrop:** it borrows War Citadel's until M6.
- **Toasts** overlap the move grid for 5 s, passing taps through.
- **A hung request** has no client timeout; the battle's `Leave` appears after 5 s of saving.
- **After a lost reply and a reload,** the rewards are kept, but the result screen doesn't show.
- **A PvP loss can be dodged** by cutting the connection, as closing the tab already could. M6 should forfeit pending offers.
- **Art notes:** `fx.move.war` hides its target for about 0.4 s. Trial Champion's idle and small UI pieces were drawn in code.
- **Landscape phone (844×390):** controls cover the bottom of the stage, including your plate.
