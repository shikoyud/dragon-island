- Terra × Flame first breed: both hybrids at 50.00 %, `1m` breed and hatch, no species name on the egg, `Hatched Flame-Terra` toast, Book shows `3 / 106`.

## Boot time with the art

Measured on the Vite dev server with the cache disabled: nine cold loads at 2.6–4.5 s, median about 3.3 s, from navigation to an interactive island. With 4× CPU throttle: 9.9–16.3 s. Each cold load fetches 367 art PNGs, 1.4 MB, about 1–2 s of the total. Acceptable on desktop. On a slow phone it is long, and no production build or real phone was measured, and there is no baseline from before the art. If phones turn out slow, a sprite atlas or loading off-screen species later would help.
