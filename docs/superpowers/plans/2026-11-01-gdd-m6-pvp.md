# M6 PvP Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the placeholder PvP with GDD §6.4–6.5: the lifetime ladder with Bastion, Arena Energy, leagues and the trophy formula, Team Power matchmaking with Ghost teams, the Daily Ladder, Ladder Lock, Grand Finals, Champion's Arena (A6), and an Arena screen.

**Architecture:** PvP adopts the Trial model from M5. Offers carry no seed. `startPvp` freezes the opponent, team and energy in `state.arena.active`, and only then can the seed be read; `finishPvp` re-simulates the moves from that snapshot. The three queues (ladder, Finals, Champion's Arena) are one flow with a `mode`. Everything that touches $DRIS goes through the existing `charge` effect, or a new mock payout ledger whose amounts are zero until M7 (A16).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, Fastify + Drizzle (PGlite in dev), React + Phaser, Playwright, pnpm workspace. Tests and the game run on shiko's PC.

**Spec:** `docs/dragon-island-gdd.md` (§ references below), with `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-gdd-rulings.md` (A# answers, R# rulings) and the roadmap `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-30-gdd-roadmap.md`.

**Unchanged inputs:** the open economy questions stay on GDD values: M4's Gold income and clear XP, M5's replay pay, counters at unlock, 3-dragon Trials at PL 4 and 1v1 turns. M3's level-up modal and Day 13 jump stay on defaults. Task 6 reports PvP's share of Gold and Food against them.

## Review Focus

1. Seeds and offers: no seed before `startPvp`; refreshing or re-fetching offers can't reveal or reroll one; an offer can't be used twice, or by another player; a client can't name its own opponent, team HP, Bastion or trophies. Pinned in Tasks 3 and 4.
2. Connection-drop dodging: a started battle not finished by its deadline is a loss for the attacker and credits the defender, even if the player never returns, across a day rollover and across Ladder Lock. Abandon is the same. Pinned in Task 4.
3. Energy: spent once at start, never refunded by loss, forfeit, replay or a rolled-back request; regen is correct across long gaps and a clock stepping back; a refill can't exceed 3 a game day or be charged twice. Pinned in Tasks 2 and 4.
4. Rank manipulation: the same two players trading wins can't climb the Daily Ladder; rollover settles once per day, even if the job runs twice or late; a banned player isn't paid; ties are stable; league floors hold on attack and defence. Pinned in Tasks 1 and 5.
5. Ghost and snapshot teams: a defender who sells, feeds or changes team after an offer fights as snapshotted; a defender deleted or reset doesn't break the attacker; Ghosts are within Team Power and deterministic per seed. Pinned in Tasks 3 and 4.
6. Determinism and fairness: the same seed gives the same defender AI on client and server with Bastion on; Team Power can't be gamed by a weak saved defence team. Pinned in Tasks 3 and 4.
